Theme 0 starts with a diagnostic. You've read Ball twice, so you probably have some vocabulary already. The diagnostic is meant to find out which parts are real understanding and which parts are familiar words you couldn't yet use.

Answer in your own words, in a few sentences each. "No idea" and "I know the word but couldn't explain it" are both useful answers, so don't look anything up. I'll correct errors bluntly and use the results to decide where to start.

**Cells and structure**

1. What's the basic difference between a bacterial cell and one of your cells? Why might that difference matter?
2. What is a cell membrane made of, and why doesn't it dissolve in water?

**Information**

3. What is DNA physically? Why does its structure suggest a way to copy it?
4. Walk through how a stretch of DNA ends up as a working protein. Name the intermediate steps and molecules you know.
5. A gene is "a sequence that codes for a protein." Is that definition accurate? If not, what does it leave out? (Ball's influence may show here, and that's fine. Say where your answer comes from.)

**Proteins**

6. A protein is a chain of amino acids. How does a one-dimensional chain end up doing something specific, like speeding up one particular reaction?

**Energy**

7. What is ATP for? Where does a cell get the energy to make it?
8. Rough sense of scale: how big is a typical protein compared with a typical cell, and how fast do things happen inside one? Order-of-magnitude guesses are fine.

**Two logistics questions**

* Do you have _Essential Cell Biology_ or _The Vital Question_ on hand, or will we work mostly from explanation here and add outside reading later?
* How much time per session or per week are you planning? Theme 0 can be a fast two-week vocabulary pass, or a slower build with problem sets.
